AI adoption works best when a legal team starts with a bounded problem, a clear owner and an honest definition of acceptable risk.

## Choose a narrow use case

Look for repetitive work where output can be reviewed and where improvement is easy to measure. Avoid beginning with the most sensitive or least understood process.

## Define the guardrails first

Agree what information may be used, who reviews the output, what must be recorded and when the tool should not be used.

## Learn before scaling

A short, structured pilot should produce evidence about quality, effort and adoption. Scale only when the evidence supports it.
